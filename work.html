Starting from nothing You have an idea and a deadline and nothing on screen. I design the first version and build enough of it that you can put it in front of a real person and find out. Start something →
Fixing what's live Something shipped and it isn't landing. I find where people are getting stuck and fix it, usually in the codebase rather than in a deck about the codebase. Send me the problem →
Two weeks of pushing No brief, just a question you can't answer. Two weeks to explore it properly, and you keep everything I make whether you use it or not. Book two weeks →
Sticking around Months rather than weeks, alongside your team. This is the one I like most, and the one where the work usually gets good. Talk about a longer run →
